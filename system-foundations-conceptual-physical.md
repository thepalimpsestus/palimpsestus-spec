**Derives from:** CL-1 (Sovereignty), CL-51 (Consequence Non-Externalizability)
**Applies to:** CC (Constitutional Root)

*My walls are mine. My doors are mine. You knock; I answer or I don't.*

**Irreducible Claim:** A containment surface whose permeability is exclusively controlled by the enclosed entity. **Irreducibility:** CP-5 (Interior-Controlled Gate) specifies how a POINT on the boundary decides to open, but not who owns the boundary itself. CP-13 (Transforming Gate) transforms what crosses, but doesn't address the question of ownership. You could have interior-controlled gates on a boundary owned by someone else — that's not sovereignty. Only CP-1 makes the boundary surface itself the exclusive property of the enclosed entity.

### CP-73. Ignorance-to-Discovery Converter

CP-19 (Absence-Powered Oscillation) uses absence as energy but doesn't produce discovery as output. ### CP-74. Structural Limitation Declaration
**Epistemic Tier:** Tier 3 (Derived Consequence)


**Derives from:** CL-30 (Humility)
**Applies to:** CC (Constitutional Root)

*I don't know, and I MUST say so. Silence about uncertainty is a lie by omission.*

**Irreducible Claim:** A mandatory disclosure mechanism attached to any decision-making node: when an agent acts on partial information, it must physically attach its uncertainty to the output. CL-30 says those wielding power must transparently admit their limitations. This produces MANDATORY UNCERTAINTY TAGGING: every decision, every edge traversal, every claim that emerges from an agent operating with incomplete information must carry a physical tag declaring what the agent DIDN'T know when it made that decision. This is not optional confidence scoring — it is a RESPONSIBILITY (CC-8) to declare what you DON'T KNOW (CC-10). Omitting the uncertainty tag is structurally equivalent to lying — performing certainty you don't possess.

**Corollaries:**
- Every output carries its own ignorance receipt: the consumer of a decision can inspect exactly what the decider didn't know.
- Cascading uncertainty: if Decision B depends on Decision A, B's uncertainty tag includes A's uncertainty tag — uncertainty compounds through derivation chains.
- An agent that claims zero uncertainty is either operating with complete information (impossible per LC-9) or lying. The system can structurally detect this lie.
- Humility is measurable: an agent's average uncertainty tag size is a structural metric of its epistemic honesty.

**Irreducibility:** CP-28 (Confidence-Scored Edge) scores the EDGE, not the AGENT — it measures relationship confidence, not the agent's epistemic state. CP-17 (Integrity-Scoring Absence) scores gaps in the TOPOLOGY, not in the agent's knowledge. Only CP-74 mandates that the AGENT itself declare what it doesn't know at the moment of action — it's about the actor's responsibility to acknowledge its own limitations, not about scoring the environment.

### CP-75. Power-Bounded Certainty Ceiling
**Epistemic Tier:** Tier 3 (Derived Consequence)


**Derives from:** CL-30 (Humility) + CL-15 (Social Contract)
**Applies to:** CC (Constitutional Root)

*The more power you wield, the MORE you must acknowledge what you don't know. Certainty is inversely proportional to influence.*

**Irreducible Claim:** A physical constraint that inversely couples an agent's influence radius to its maximum allowable certainty claim. CL-30 demands structural humility from power-holders. CL-15 demands that those holding power protect the rights of others. Together: an agent whose decisions affect many other nodes is STRUCTURALLY PREVENTED from claiming high certainty. The wider your blast radius, the lower your certainty ceiling. This is because high-certainty claims from high-influence agents suppress the questioning engine (CP-72) in their vicinity — if the powerful are "certain," why would anyone question? Power combined with certainty is the physical shape of intellectual tyranny.

**Corollaries:**
- System-level decisions carry the lowest certainty ceilings — global policies must be expressed with maximum acknowledged uncertainty.
- Local decisions (affecting only the agent itself) can carry higher certainty because the blast radius is contained.
- An agent that accumulates influence (more edges, more dependents) sees its certainty ceiling DROP automatically — growing power structurally forces growing humility.
- This prevents epistemic monocultures: no single high-influence agent can impose certainty on the graph, because its certainty claims are structurally capped.

**Irreducibility:** CP-74 (Structural Limitation Declaration) requires agents to DECLARE uncertainty but doesn't CAP it based on influence. An agent could declare "I'm 99% certain" while affecting millions of nodes — CP-74 would accept that declaration as honest. CP-75 says the declaration must be BOUNDED: if your influence is high, you CANNOT claim 99% certainty, even if you believe it. CP-28 (Confidence-Scored Edge) scores edges neutrally without considering the power differential between the agents. Only CP-75 couples the certainty ceiling to influence radius.
